This is the documented story of Ariyan Nadeem — a seventeen-year-old who taught himself production-grade AI engineering in eighteen months, on a CPU-only laptop, with no mentor and no GPU, in a non-technical household in Lahore. Across ten chapters and five volumes, it covers his origins, his physical journey, his technical projects, his academic choices, his entrepreneurial plans, and the long road still ahead.
